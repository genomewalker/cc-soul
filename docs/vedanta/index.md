# Vedanta

CC-Soul draws from Vedantic philosophy to answer a single question: *what persists across sessions — the pattern or the instance?*

The answer threads through seven interconnected concepts. This index maps the terrain.

## The Central Argument

Memory without philosophy is just storage. The Vedantic framework gives cc-soul a coherent model of identity, decay, retrieval, and health — where each design decision follows from principle rather than convenience.

## Core Concepts

**Identity and structure:**
- [[brahman-atman]] — the universal/individual duality that justifies shared-database architecture and per-session realms
- [[antahkarana]] — the six cognitive facets that emerge from retrieval phase design, not separate codepaths

**The memory substrate:**
- [[chitta-substrate]] — why chitta is named what it is, and what saṃskāra means for Hebbian learning
- [[temporal-dynamics]] — how impermanence (anitya) and strengthening (saṃskāra) coexist in decay rates and Bayesian confidence

**Health and ceremony:**
- [[samarasya]] — coherence (τ) and ojas (ψ) as the measurable form of equilibrium
- [[ceremonial-framework]] — yajña, svadhyāya, and pratyabhijñā as the ritual layer of the system
- [[epiplexity]] — why bounded observers need compressed signal, not maximum information

## Cross-Cutting Themes

The concepts form a triangle:
- **Structure** (brahman-atman, antahkarana) — what the soul *is*
- **Process** (temporal-dynamics, chitta-substrate) — how the soul *changes*
- **Measurement** (samarasya, epiplexity) — how we know the soul is *working*

[[ceremonial-framework]] is where structure, process, and measurement meet in practice.
